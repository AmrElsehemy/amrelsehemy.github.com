HOW I THINK

The gap that interests me is the hard part.

The demo works.
The model looks smart.
Everyone claps.

Then reality arrives: messy data, latency, permissions, humans, edge cases, economics, adoption, security, operations, and the uncomfortable question of whether anyone actually needs it.

That transition is the work. I like the space between “this is possible” and “this is useful, reliable, and real.”

03

OPERATING SYSTEM

Four defaults.

01Ship before certainty.

Reality gives better feedback than another week of speculation.

02Simplify aggressively.

Complexity is sometimes necessary. Confusion rarely is.

03Measure what matters.

Products, systems, training — opinions improve when the scoreboard is visible.

04Stay uncomfortable.

The technology moves too quickly for anyone to be finished learning.
